Stem

STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics)

The STEM strand at Rosales National High School (RNHS) is designed for students passionate about science, mathematics, technology, and engineering. This strand prepares students for careers in engineering, medicine, IT, research, and more through critical thinking, problem-solving, and innovation.

Core Subjects

General Physics 1 & 2
General Chemistry 1 & 2
General Biology 1 & 2
Pre-Calculus and Basic Calculus
Statistics and Probability
Earth and Life Science
Disaster Readiness and Risk Reduction

Skills and Competencies

The STEM strand hones skills in research, scientific experimentation, and technological innovation. Analytical skills, logical reasoning, and mathematical foundations are key components of this program.

Why Choose STEM?

STEM prepares students for careers that drive innovation. It fosters curiosity, leadership, and perseverance, equipping students to contribute to society and shape the future.
