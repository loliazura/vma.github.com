TVL

TVL (Technical-Vocational-Livelihood)

The TVL strand offers various specializations for students interested in technical and vocational careers. TVL prepares students for immediate employment, entrepreneurship, or further training in their chosen fields.

Information and Communication Technology (ICT)

Computer Systems Servicing
Web Development
Programming
Networking

ICT focuses on computer hardware, software, and programming, preparing students for careers in tech support, development, and IT services.

Home Economics (HE)

Food and Beverage Services
Bread and Pastry Production
Housekeeping
Caregiving

HE offers practical skills in culinary arts, hospitality, and caregiving, preparing students for careers in the food industry and service sectors.

Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)

Basic Welding Techniques
Advanced Welding Techniques
Safety Practices
Fabrication

SMAW teaches students welding skills for construction, repair, and industrial work, focusing on safety and precision.

Dressmaking

Pattern Making
Sewing Techniques
Clothing Design
Alterations and Repairs

Dressmaking focuses on creating garments, teaching design, sewing, and tailoring skills for students pursuing careers in fashion or custom tailoring.

Why Choose TVL?

TVL is ideal for students who prefer hands-on learning and want to be job-ready after Senior High School. It also serves as a pathway for those who plan to continue technical education or entrepreneurship in specialized fields.
