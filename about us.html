About Us

Welcome To RNHS!

Rosales National High School (RNHS) is a high school in Rosales, Pangasinan, Philippines. It's known for offering quality education that helps students grow academically and personally. The school provides different tracks and strands to cater to various interests and career goals. These include the Academic Track, where students can choose from strands like STEM, ABM, and HUMSS, as well as the Technical-Vocational Livelihood (TVL) Track, which gives students practical skills for their future jobs.
