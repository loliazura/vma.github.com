ABM (Accountancy, Business, and Management)

The ABM strand at Rosales National High School (RNHS) is designed for students interested in business, finance, marketing, and entrepreneurship. This strand prepares students for careers in accountancy, management, sales, and business development through leadership, financial literacy, and strategic decision-making skills.

Core Subjects

Fundamentals of Accountancy, Business, and Management 1 & 2
Business Mathematics
Organization and Management
Principles of Marketing
Business Finance
Applied Economics
Business Ethics and Social Responsibility

Skills and Competencies

The ABM strand hones skills in critical thinking, financial analysis, and entrepreneurial decision-making. Leadership, teamwork, and effective communication are key competencies developed through project-based learning and case studies.

Why Choose ABM?

Choosing the ABM strand opens doors to a variety of career opportunities in the corporate world. It’s ideal for students who dream of becoming entrepreneurs, business owners, accountants, or corporate leaders. ABM fosters creativity, financial awareness, and leadership skills—qualities essential for thriving in a competitive business environment.
